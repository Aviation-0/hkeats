How EatSafe HK Verifies Food Hygiene Licences

Direct FEHD Synchronization

We synchronize statutory records directly from the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD) covering Restaurant Licences, Bakeries, Factory Canteens, and Restricted Food Permits.

Concessions & Dai Pai Dongs

Fine dining venues in luxury hotels (e.g. Amber in Landmark Mandarin) operate under valid master hotel licences. Iconic street stalls (e.g. Sing Heung Yuen) are verified under Cap. 132AI Hawker Pitch Regulations.

Strict Spatial Geofencing

Our algorithm verifies micro-district boundaries and prevents cross-harbour false matches, ensuring you always see the authentic licence for the exact premises you are visiting.

Chrome Extension Available

Get Licence Cards Directly in Google Maps

When you click any Hong Kong restaurant on Google Maps, EatSafe HK docks alongside Google's sidebar, instantly displaying its FEHD licence type, licence details, and any suspension orders.
